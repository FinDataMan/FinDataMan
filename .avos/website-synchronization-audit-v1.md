# Website Synchronization Audit v1

**Project:** ARTURO-PERSONAL-001  
**Purpose:** Convert the current site into a public representation governed by the same personal nodes used for LinkedIn and GitHub.

## Executive finding

The current website is technically usable but strategically outdated. It should be treated as a historical implementation layer rather than a source of truth.

## Keep

- Static, lightweight deployment approach unless implementation evidence justifies a framework migration.
- Existing repository and public continuity.
- Direct contact paths, subject to privacy and accuracy review.
- Clear sections for profile, experience, capabilities and contact.

## Update

- Site identity from a narrow FinDataMan persona toward Arturo Villagomez's governed personal identity.
- Positioning to finance, operations and applied AI.
- Hero promise and narrative hierarchy.
- Experience titles, dates and descriptions using approved LinkedIn nodes.
- Skills into evidence-backed capabilities.
- Visual system to the approved Arturo Villagomez identity.
- Metadata, accessibility, mobile behavior and link integrity.
- Contact strategy and primary CTA.

## Remove or replace

- Unsupported superlatives and claims.
- Outdated role descriptions.
- Statements that imply ownership, exclusivity or market-first status without approved evidence.
- Legacy project references that are not explicitly approved for publication.
- Copy that presents Arturo only as a financial analyst.
- Third-party logos where permission, accuracy or current relevance is uncertain.

## Validate before publication

- Every quantified claim.
- Current role names and dates.
- Public use of client, employer and project names.
- WhatsApp number and email exposure.
- Project ownership and contribution wording.
- Whether the public brand should lead with Arturo Villagomez, AV monogram or FinDataMan as a legacy label.
- Domain and deployment target.

## Proposed v1 architecture

1. Hero: identity, value and primary action.
2. What I build: finance, operations and applied AI systems.
3. Selected work: only approved projects and evidence.
4. Experience: concise trajectory derived from canonical LinkedIn nodes.
5. Capabilities: reusable abilities rather than software keyword lists.
6. Operating principles: systems, evidence, clarity and human authorship.
7. Contact: one clear next action plus professional channels.
