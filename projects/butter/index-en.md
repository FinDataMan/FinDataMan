# Butter

> What if the business changes?

Financial logic and product experience to explore decisions

Co-founder · Financial logic · Product experience

Work carried out with collaborators. I distinguish delivered features from improvements we are still testing.

## The challenge

Business leaders need to explore what might happen if sales, costs or targets change, keep alternatives and compare them with actual performance. The challenge was to turn those questions into an understandable financial experience.

## My contribution

As co-founder, I defined the rules, logic and experience design for budgets, scenarios and actual-versus-plan comparisons. I worked on organising actions, presenting variances and connecting users’ questions to the financial context available to the AI assistant.

## Outcome and scope

My contribution focused on turning financial judgement into product behaviour and requirements for the development team. Implementation involved collaborators; I distinguish delivered features from improvements we are still testing.
