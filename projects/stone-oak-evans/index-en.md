# Stone Oak & Evans

> Information to assess an opportunity

Identity and digital experience for informed enquiries

Identity · Digital experience · Technical SEO

The examples illustrate communication and the digital experience. They contain no commercial terms or private financial information.

## The challenge

To assess a real estate opportunity, people need to understand its location, characteristics and surroundings, and know how to explore further. The challenge was to organise that information into a coherent experience in English and Spanish.

## My contribution

I led the identity and digital experience: editorial design, property information, maps, contextual photography and enquiry journeys. The scope included technical SEO, structured data, image, font and stylesheet optimisation, and consent controls for analytics.

## Outcome and scope

The solution brings location, visual and commercial information together in an accessible structure. My contribution was to connect the audience’s needs with content, design and development decisions, preserving source accuracy and making the limits of the information clear.
