# Financial system for my own practice

> From data to decisions you can explain

Designed and developed with AI to deepen financial analysis

Financial judgement · Product · Applied AI

A tool for my own work, with functions I continue to develop and test. Company data remains private. This is not presented as a commercially available product.

## The challenge

Reconciliation, consolidation and planning require context. I need to answer questions from management, teams and other stakeholders at companies where I work or serve as CFO, without rebuilding each analysis from scratch.

## My contribution

I designed and developed an AI-assisted system for my own practice: reconciliation, consolidation, planning, modelling and scenarios. I translated financial methodology into modules with calculation rules, versions, permissions and tests. A context-aware assistant supports deeper analysis while preserving the links between sources, assumptions and results.

## Outcome and scope

The system brings together tools to prepare analyses and address questions from management, internal teams and other stakeholders. My contribution combines financial judgement, design and AI-assisted development. I continue to develop and test its functions in my own working environment, documenting what has been checked and what still needs testing.

## Boundaries

A tool for my own work, with functions I continue to develop and test. Company data remains private. This is not presented as a commercially available product.
