### Don Ventas

> Clarify the offer and measure the journey

Strategy, content and web design to guide the next step

Strategy · Content · Web design · Analytics

Measurement configuration and testing, plus a reporting trial. No increases in sales or conversion are claimed without evidence.

#### The challenge

People seeking support for their brand need to recognise whether an offer fits their situation, understand the process and know what to do next. The challenge was to build that journey and define how to observe its friction points.

#### My contribution

I developed the offering strategy, brand system, site architecture and content around audience questions. I worked on how the site appears in search results and directed tests to measure visits and steps before an enquiry, using Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager.

#### Outcome and scope

I also designed a reporting trial and rules to distinguish visits, enquiries, opportunities and commercial outcomes. The work established a communication and measurement foundation with quality controls and documented open items. My contribution connects design, content and analytics with the decisions the business needs to make.

#### Boundaries

Measurement configuration and testing, plus a reporting trial. No increases in sales or conversion are claimed without evidence.
